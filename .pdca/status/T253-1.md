The same new test must fail against the original renderer and pass with the fix. Proof: **R-red**, **R-green**, including exit codes and actual failure.

Negative case: null must not become zero or throw. Synthetic integer-boundary tests demonstrate and documentation discloses `toInt32` wrapping, rather than promising checked overflow. Proof: **U**, **I**.
