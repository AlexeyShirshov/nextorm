## Root cause
Verified chain: `src/nextorm.core/Builders/EntityBuilder.cs:331,343` → `src/nextorm.core/Query/QueryCommand.cs:263` → `src/nextorm.core/Query/QueryCommand.QueryPreparer.cs:513-519,722-725,135,75` → `src/nextorm.core/Visitors/TypeFacts.cs:72-82` → `src/nextorm.core/DataContext/RowMapperFactory.cs:301-321,348` → `src/nextorm.core/DataContext/RowMaterializerBuilder.cs:69,312-315`. The scalar enum misses primitive classification, reaches complex materialization, and fails ctor discovery. The DateTimeOffset guard is at `src/nextorm.core/Visitors/MemberTranslator.cs:177-205`, with its only call site at `:22`; bare scalar classification bypasses it.

## Decision (r1)

Frozen insertion: `QueryCommand.QueryPreparer.cs`, immediately before the original `:722-725` classification gate, after converter resolution at original `:562-564`.
Order: (1) `MemberTranslator.ThrowIfUnsupportedDateTimeOffsetAccess(body)`; (2) refuse enum or nullable-enum `body.Type` only when `bodyConverter is null`, using E; (3) unchanged classification gate.
Do not broaden rejection to enum properties inside whole-entity/DTO/tuple projections.
