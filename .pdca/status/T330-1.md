## 5. Minimal solution and alternatives

**Essence:** remove the mandatory source argument for the common bare-source case.

**Constraints:** retain existing overloads, resolution semantics, validation contracts, constraints and provider behavior; reject null predicates before source construction.

**Choice:** add all five counterparts. Each performs the necessary early predicate validation, then delegates through `_dataProvider.From<TChild>()` to the corresponding existing overload. Preserve existing generic parameters, including any key-type parameters. Do not introduce a shared resolver.

| Approach | Benefit | Cost / risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Five thin validating wrappers | Small change; explicit equivalence; no new provider implementation | Repeated small predicate guard; compiler-binding verification required | **Chosen** |
| Shared validation/source-construction seam | Removes a little repetition | Broadens internal change and may reorder validation or resolution | Reject unless D1 demonstrates a concrete necessity; return to PLAN before widening |
| Fold into T331 | Fewer separately tracked changes | Loses predicate-only usage; couples unrelated ON synthesis | Reject |

Also rejected:

- Optional `child` parameter: cannot provide the requested leading-argument omission without a different overload shape and introduces binding risks.
- Changing existing signatures: breaks callers.
- New resolver or fallback table: violates mapping and failure parity.
- Shipping only collection forms: leaves an avoidable symmetry gap; all five share the same bounded delegation approach.
